# Owner flows

Owner = property owner, housing association board or facility manager. The owner's list of spaces and tenants is the source of truth for who may share which space. In the pilot, ParkShare24 admins do most of this for the owner.

## O1. Agreement and car park setup (pilot, done with admin)
1. Owner signs the agreement with **MitID Erhverv**. The agreement gives the subletting consent and the 40/20/40 split (tenants/owner/ParkShare24).
2. Create the car park: address, entrance, opening hours, height limit.
3. Add spaces: number, level, type (EV charging, wide, disabled).
4. Choose which spaces can be shared. ParkShare24 sets the price: always the cheapest in the area (see decisions).
5. Install cameras and signs (ParkShare24).

## O2. Link tenants to spaces (pilot)
1. Enter or **import** tenants: name, phone or email, space number. Big owners already have this in their rental system, so a spreadsheet import is required, not just one-by-one entry.
2. Send invitations (starts tenant flow T1).
3. Approve tenants whose MitID name does not match (e.g. a partner).

## O3. Overview
- Status per space: not invited / invited / linked / sharing.
- Bookings, occupancy, revenue and the owner's 20 %.
- Pilot: a monthly report by email. In-app dashboard later.

## O4. Keep it up to date
- Tenant moves out: owner removes the link. Sharing stops, outstanding earnings are paid out, the space becomes vacant.
- New tenant: link and invite.
- Owner can report a wrong link, and a tenant can report "that's not my space" to the owner from the app. The owner's list decides.

## O5. Rules
- Which spaces are shared, set hours, price.
- Blocked plates.
