# Admin flows (ParkShare24 staff)

## A1. Onboard a car park
Do owner flows O1 and O2 on the owner's behalf: create the car park and spaces, import tenants, send invitations, resend invitations.

## A2. Support
- Look up a booking, driver, tenant or space.
- Fix tenant–space links (with the owner's approval).
- Open the gate remotely for a stuck driver.
- Handle the "tenant came home early" case (T4) and pay compensation.

## A3. Money
- Refunds.
- Monthly payouts to tenants and owners.
- Reports for owners (pilot: monthly email).

## A4. Faults
- Alerts when a camera or gate is offline, or plate recognition fails repeatedly.
- Pause bookings for a car park while it is down.
