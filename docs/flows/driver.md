# Driver flows

The driver flows are where the money comes in. Booking must take under a minute from opening the app.

## D1. Sign up
1. Phone number (SMS code).
2. Add license plate(s).
3. Add payment method (MobilePay, card). The card is saved so extensions and agreed extra time can be charged.
4. Accept terms, including the camera surveillance notice and the overstay terms (see D4).

## D2. Find and book a space (pilot)
1. Search an address or use the map. Show car parks with free spaces for the chosen time.
2. Choose start and end time. Show total price before paying.
3. Show the car park's entry details: address, entrance, height limit.
4. Pay up front. Confirmation shows time, address and how to get in.
5. Only times where a space is guaranteed free (incl. the tenant's buffer) can be booked.

## D3. Arrive and get in (pilot)
1. The camera reads the plate at the entrance; the barrier/gate opens.
2. The app shows where to park: level, zone, space number (or "any free shared space" if spaces are pooled).
3. **Fallback if the plate is not recognised:** an "Åbn port" ("Open gate") button in the app, active only during the booking at that car park, plus a support phone number. Never leave a driver stuck at the barrier.
4. Exit: camera reads the plate; the barrier opens.

## D4. Extend, end or cancel
- Reminder 15 minutes before the booking ends, with a one-tap extend (if the space is still free).
- End early: no refund by default (to decide, see open questions).
- Cancel: free up to a set time before start (to decide).

## D5. Overstay
Fines may not be sent afterwards, so overstays must be prevented, not punished:
1. Reminders before the end time.
2. Easy extension.
3. If the driver stays anyway: charge the extra time to the saved card, under terms the driver accepted at sign-up. **Must be cleared by the lawyer** so it is not treated as after-the-fact charging (efteropkrævning).
4. If the overstay blocks a returning tenant: see tenant flow T4.
